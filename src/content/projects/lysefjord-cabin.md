## The brief

A young family wanted a cabin that sleeps eight, survives muddy boots, and still feels calm on a grey November Sunday.

## What we did

- One oak wall does everything: bunks, storage, bench, hidden desk
- Wet-room entry with heated floor keeps the living zone clean
- Lighting on three circuits — work, dinner, fire-gazing
- All materials chosen to grey gracefully, not to be repainted

## Outcome

The cabin now rents out 20+ weeks a year when the family isn't there — the interior photographs so well it markets itself.
