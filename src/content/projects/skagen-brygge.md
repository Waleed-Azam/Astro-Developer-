## The brief

A waterfront hotel with great bones and tired rooms. The owners wanted a full refresh without closing for a season — so we phased the work floor by floor, keeping 70% of rooms bookable throughout.

## What we did

- Re-planned 42 rooms around three repeatable typologies (fewer SKUs, faster install)
- Designed a ground-floor restaurant that works from breakfast to late bar
- Specified daylight-first materials: lime wash, oiled oak, brushed brass
- Documented everything in a FF&E bible the in-house team still uses

## Outcome

Occupancy rose **18%** year over year in the first season, and the restaurant now runs at a profit on weekdays — previously weekends only.

> "They think like operators, not just designers. Every choice had a reason we could explain to the board." — Hotel director
