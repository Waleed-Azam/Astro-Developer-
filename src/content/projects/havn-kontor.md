## The brief

Havn outgrew two floors of open plan. Noise complaints were constant, and the best engineers were booking phone booths for entire days.

## What we did

- Mapped a week of real behaviour (badges + interviews, not surveys alone)
- Zoned the floor into library-quiet, team-loud and client-facing bands
- Built 14 focus rooms from one repeatable timber module
- Added acoustic ceilings tuned per zone — see our [acoustics guide](/journal/office-acoustics-that-work)

## Outcome

Meeting-room complaints fell **70%**. Desk utilisation is up because people actually want to come in.
