Every noisy office asks for the same fix: more pods. Pods help — but they're the aspirin, not the cure.

## Zone before you furnish

Divide the floor into three bands:

1. **Library** — no calls, ever. Felt, bookshelves, soft floor.
2. **Team** — normal collaboration volume. This is the default.
3. **Loud** — sales calls, standups, the coffee machine.

Put the loud band furthest from the library, with team space as a buffer. At [Havn Kontor](/work/havn-kontor) this single move cut complaints by 70% before we bought anything.

## Measure twice

A phone decibel app is enough for zoning decisions. Aim for under 45 dB in library zones during a normal workday. If you can't hit it, the zone boundary is in the wrong place.

## Spend where ears are

One acoustic ceiling over the loud band beats three designer pods. Soft furnishings at ear height (1.1–1.7 m) beat flooring upgrades. Felt on 20% of wall area is the sweet spot — more is diminishing returns.
