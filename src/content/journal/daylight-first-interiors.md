In Stavanger we get 18-hour summer days and 6-hour winter ones. Designing for the *average* fails both seasons — so we design for the extremes.

## 1. Map the sun path, not the floor plan

Before sketching, we photograph the site at 9:00, 12:00 and 16:00 on an overcast day. Overcast light is the honest baseline: if a corner works in November grey, it works year-round.

## 2. Put the long-stay functions in the best light

Desks, reading chairs and kitchen tables claim the brightest walls. Storage, bathrooms and corridors take what's left. This sounds obvious; most plans do the opposite because plumbing is easier to cluster.

## 3. Bounce, don't just admit

Pale ceilings, a mirror opposite the window, one glossy surface per room. Together they lift the back of a 6-metre room by roughly a third — measured with a €40 lux meter, not guessed.

> Rule of thumb: if you need artificial light at noon in June, the plan is wrong — not the lamp.
